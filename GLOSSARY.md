# Cadence

A course platform where Students buy Courses and work through their Lessons.

## Language

### Refunds

**Refund Request**:
A Student's request to get back the amount they paid for a Course. Cadence approves or declines it, and approving it ends the Student's access to the Course.
_Avoid_: Cancellation, Reversal, Chargeback

**Open Refund Request**:
A Refund Request that has been neither approved nor declined. A Purchase has at most one at a time, and a declined Refund Request does not stop the Student from asking again.
_Avoid_: Pending, Requested

**Refund**:
The money Cadence sends back to a Student when their Refund Request is approved.

**Refund Window**:
The period after a Purchase in which a Student can make a Refund Request.
_Avoid_: Cancel window, Cancellation window
